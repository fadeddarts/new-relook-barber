How to use the board

Customers book an appointment, not a chair. They pick a time on the website, then send WhatsApp. Nothing is held until you reply OK and add the name here.

Open the board

1. Open Staff login

On the website tap Staff login (on a phone it is inside Menu). Type the PIN you were given. The PIN is not on the public site.

2. When a WhatsApp comes in

  1. Read the name, service, day, and time.
  2. If that time still works, reply OK on WhatsApp.
  3. On the board: type the name, pick the same service (add extras if they bundled), pick the day and time, tap Save.

Haircut & beard, or any mix over 30 minutes, holds two tiles (for example 10:00 and 10:30). You only tap the start time. Walk-ins: leave the name as Walk-in, pick the service and time, Save.

3. What the customer sees

Book only shows times that still have room. When every opening at that time is filled, the time disappears from Book. Two people can still WhatsApp the same slot until you fill it.

4. How many barbers

Default is 2. Use + / − then Save. That number is the shop default from then on (max 4), not only the day on screen. If a day already has more names in one slot than the new number, that day stays high enough until you Remove those names.

5. Cancel

Customer sends a cancel WhatsApp. You tap Remove on that name. The time opens on Book again.

6. Sign out

Tap Logout when you are done. The board also signs out after 30 minutes with no tap or key. You will need the PIN again.

Phone, tablet, computer

The form is one column on every size: name, service, day, times, how many barbers, Save, then today’s names. Light / Dark is in the top bar.
